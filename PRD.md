# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

## Title: Health and Lifestyle Issues Among College Students

### 1. Problem Statement

College students often face health and lifestyle problems because of irregular food habits, lack of physical activity, insufficient sleep, academic pressure, and poor time management. These problems can affect their physical health, mental well-being, academic performance, and daily life.

### 2. Target Users

The main users are **college students**, especially students who have difficulty maintaining a healthy and balanced lifestyle while managing their studies.

### 3. Objectives

The main objectives are:

* To identify common health and lifestyle problems among college students.
* To encourage students to follow healthy food habits.
* To promote regular physical activity.
* To encourage students to get sufficient sleep and rest.
* To create awareness about cleanliness and personal hygiene.
* To help students maintain a better balance between studies and personal health.

### 4. Major Problems

The five major health and lifestyle problems are:

1. **Unhealthy Eating Habits** – Skipping meals or frequently eating unhealthy food.
2. **Lack of Exercise** – Spending long hours sitting and studying without enough physical activity.
3. **Insufficient Sleep** – Staying awake late for studying, assignments, or entertainment.
4. **Stress and Academic Pressure** – Feeling stressed because of exams, assignments, and academic responsibilities.
5. **Poor Hygiene and Cleanliness** – Not maintaining proper personal and environmental cleanliness.

### 5. Proposed Solution

A **Student Health and Lifestyle Management System** can be developed to help students maintain healthy daily habits.

The system can provide:

* Daily health reminders.
* Exercise reminders.
* Healthy eating suggestions.
* Sleep and rest reminders.
* Personal hygiene reminders.
* Simple daily activity tracking.
* Basic health and lifestyle awareness information.

### 6. Key Features

| Feature           | Purpose                                   |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Daily Reminder    | Reminds students about healthy activities |
| Exercise Tracker  | Helps students track physical activity    |
| Food Suggestions  | Provides simple healthy food choices      |
| Sleep Reminder    | Encourages proper sleep and rest          |
| Hygiene Reminder  | Promotes cleanliness                      |
| Progress Tracking | Shows daily or weekly healthy habits      |

### 7. Functional Requirements

The system should allow students to:

* Create a basic student profile.
* Set daily health reminders.
* Record exercise activities.
* Record sleep hours.
* Track daily healthy habits.
* View health and lifestyle tips.
* Check their weekly progress.

### 8. Non-Functional Requirements

The system should be:

* Easy to use.
* Simple and student-friendly.
* Fast and responsive.
* Secure.
* Accessible on common devices.
* Easy to maintain.

### 9. Expected Benefits

The proposed system can help students:

* Develop healthier daily habits.
* Become more physically active.
* Maintain regular sleep.
* Improve awareness of personal hygiene.
* Manage their daily routine better.
* Reduce unhealthy lifestyle habits.

### 10. Future Scope

In the future, the system can be improved by adding:

* AI-based personalized health suggestions.
* Mobile application support.
* Smartwatch or fitness-band integration.
* Detailed activity reports.
* Healthy meal planning.
* Personalized reminders.

### 11. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if students are able to:

* Track their daily healthy activities.
* Follow regular exercise and sleep routines.
* Improve their awareness of healthy eating and hygiene.
* Monitor their lifestyle progress easily.

### 12. Conclusion

Health and lifestyle issues are important problems faced by college students. A simple **Student Health and Lifestyle Management System** can help students become more aware of their daily habits and encourage them to follow a healthier and more balanced lifestyle.
